# WebGPU Water project demo

This folder contains the project-owned showcase for the vendored
`com.abstractocclusion.webgpuwater` 1.0.0 package.

Open `Scenes/WebGpuWaterInteractivePool.unity` and enter Play Mode.

- Drag on the water to inject ripples and spray.
- Drag outside the pool to orbit; use the mouse wheel to zoom.
- Press Space to pause/resume the simulation and hold L to align the sun to the view.
- Press R to drop all floating props again; press A to toggle automatic ripples (off by default).

The scene is generated by
`AbstractOcclusion.WebGpuWater.Editor.WebGpuWaterProjectDemoBuilder`. Use
`Window > AbstractOcclusion > WebGpuWater > Rebuild Project Demo` to rebuild it.

The PC URP asset already supplies depth and opaque textures. The builder installs the
self-gating underwater-fog and caustic-projection features on `PC_Renderer.asset`.
They enqueue no passes in scenes without an active WebGPU Water body.
